\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This paper consolidates the theoretical and observational interface between the non-associative phase-space topology of the EABC framework and precision spectroscopy in atomic hydrogen. At the algebraic level, the operator sector is formally closed over $\mathbb{Q}$: for an orthogonal pair of unit octonions $e_1, e_2 \in \mathbb{O}$, the normalized squared associator $P_A = \frac{1}{4}K_{12}$ defines the exact orthogonal projector onto the orthogonal complement of the quaternion subalgebra $\mathbb{H}_{12}$, with uniform spectrum $\operatorname{spec}(K_{12}) = \{0^4, 4^4\}$. Under the automorphism group $G_2 = \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{O})$, the first-order group twirl yields the isotropic invariant $\mathcal{M}_1 = 0_{\mathbb{R}1} \oplus \frac{4}{7}I_7$, causing the non-associative origin to degenerate in first-order perturbation theory into an effective scalar shift $\eta_A^{\rm eff} = \frac{4}{7}\eta_A$. The genuine Fano tensor geometry survives exclusively in the second moment $\left.\mathcal{M}_2\right|_{\operatorname{Sym}^2(7)} = \frac{4}{7}\Pi_{\mathbf{1}} \oplus \frac{22}{63}\Pi_{\mathbf{27}}$, whereas the discrete set of seven coordinate Fano projectors is proved to fail the spherical 2-design condition.

To enable empirical falsification, we establish two complementary experimental pathways: \textbf{Path~I} serves as a quantitative precision filter. By integrating the microwave Lamb shift ($2S_{1/2} - 2P_{1/2}$), the near-degeneracy of the optical $2S\text{--}1S$ and $\text{Ly-}\alpha$ channels is geometrically lifted ($\kappa_2(F) \sim 10^7$), yielding a robust model forecast of $\sigma_{\rm forecast}(\eta_A^{\rm eff}) \lesssim 1.2 \times 10^{-13}$. \textbf{Path~II} targets the internal $\mathbf{27}_{G_2}$ tensor mode as a structural falsifier via Doppler-free $1S \to 2S$ two-photon spectroscopy under orthogonal linear polarization ($\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_1 \perp \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_2$). The coupling is fixed without ad-hoc parameters via the Clebsch-Gordan intertwiner $\Xi : \mathbf{27}_{G_2} \to \operatorname{Sym}_0^2(\mathbb{R}^3)$ with trace weight $\operatorname{Tr}(\Xi \mathcal{M}_2) = \frac{110}{63}$. Accounting for the irreducible relativistic $E1\text{--}M1$ background of the Standard Model, sensitivity reaches $\eta_A \lesssim 10^{-2}$ in the pure null channel (scaling as $\eta_A^4$) and $\sim 3 \times 10^{-3}$ in the $45^\circ$ coherent interference channel. This formulation strictly separates mathematical theorems from experimental forecasts and structural falsification.
\end{abstract}
