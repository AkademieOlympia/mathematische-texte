Dear Editors of *Communications in Mathematical Physics*,

We submit the manuscript **“EABC: Exceptional Symmetry, Lattice Geometry, and Geometric Regularity Gates”** for consideration in *Communications in Mathematical Physics*.

The manuscript develops a layered mathematical architecture linking exceptional algebraic structures, finite-dimensional information geometry, and conditional geometric regularity mechanisms. A central methodological feature is the explicit separation of four epistemic levels throughout the paper: exact algebraic theorems, standard analytic implications, finite-dimensional or numerical model evidence, and open dynamical bridges. We regard this demarcation not as a limitation of presentation but as one of the main structural contributions of the work.

Among the exact results, the paper proves the fourth-moment identity for the 240 roots of the \(E_8\) root system,
\[
\sum_{r\in R(E_8)}(r\cdot k)^4=36|k|^4,
\qquad k\in\mathbb R^8,
\]
and its dimensionally consistent pullback to any isometrically embedded physical three-plane,
\[
\iota:\mathbb R^3\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^8,
\qquad
q_r=\iota^T r,
\]
for which
\[
\sum_{r\in R(E_8)}(q_r\cdot\kappa)^4=36|\kappa|^4,
\qquad
\kappa\in\mathbb R^3.
\]
Consequently, the associated quadratic differential form
\[
\mathcal Q_{E_8}[\xi]
=
\sum_{r\in R(E_8)}
\|(q_r\cdot\nabla)^2\xi\|_{L^2}^2
\]
has the exact isotropic representation
\[
\mathcal Q_{E_8}[\xi]
=
36\|D^2\xi\|_{L^2}^2.
\]

The manuscript then isolates the precise analytic point at which this exact lattice identity would acquire PDE content. If a positive multiple
\[
\gamma\mathcal Q_{E_8}[\xi],
\qquad
\gamma>0,
\]
is generated by the effective dynamics and is controlled uniformly in time together with the lower-order norms required for localization, then standard Sobolev–Morrey theory gives local \(H^2\to W^{1,6}\to C^{0,1/2}\) control of the direction field. This is presented explicitly as a **conditional regularity gate**, not as a solution of the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes regularity problem. The positive coupling \(\gamma\), the nonlinear a-priori estimate, and the full arithmetic-to-spatial identification remain open.

A second exact component concerns the quaternionic restriction of the octonionic/Fano three-form. On a selected associative three-plane,
\[
\phi|_P=\sigma\,\mathrm{vol}_P,
\qquad
\sigma\in\{\pm1\},
\]
and for unit vectors \(\xi,\eta\in P\),
\[
\|\phi(\xi,\eta,\cdot)\|
=
|\xi\times\eta|
=
|\sin\theta|.
\]
This gives an exact algebraic direction-coherence detector while leaving open the independent physical identification of the selected associative three-plane with the spatial vorticity sector and the dynamical preservation of that sector.

The revised manuscript also includes a finite-dimensional information-geometric layer. For the normalized shape-density model
\[
\rho(A)=\frac{AA^T}{\operatorname{Tr}(AA^T)},
\]
the isotropic point is stationary, and along symmetric trace-free directions \(S\in\operatorname{Sym}_0(8)\) the local relative-entropy Hessian satisfies
\[
D''_S(0)=\frac12\|S\|_F^2.
\]
In the finite commuting sector, Umegaki relative entropy reduces exactly to classical Kullback–Leibler divergence after simultaneous diagonalization. The associated Lean 4 files are included only as **formalization targets**: they intentionally contain open proof obligations and are not presented as completed machine-checked proofs. Likewise, no derivation of an Einstein–Hilbert action, no parameter-free value of \(G_{\rm eff}\), and no unification of \(G_{\rm eff}\) with the fluid coupling \(\gamma_{\rm PDE}\) is claimed.

The paper therefore makes a deliberately narrow distinction between what is established and what is proposed as a research program. In particular:

- the \(E_8\) fourth-moment identity and the three-dimensional pullback are exact;
- the associated quartic differential symbol is exactly isotropic;
- the Fano angle identity on an associative three-plane is exact;
- the local information-geometric Hessian in the stated shape model is exact;
- the Sobolev–Morrey implications are standard analysis under the stated hypotheses;
- the emergence of a positive dynamical coupling and its uniform nonlinear control are open;
- the metric-to-operator map and any Einstein–Hilbert infrared limit are open;
- no global three-dimensional Navier–Stokes regularity theorem and no derivation of gravity from \(E_8\) are claimed.

We believe this structure may be of interest to readers of *Communications in Mathematical Physics* because it brings together exceptional Lie and lattice geometry, non-associative structures, information-geometric Hessians, and a sharply delimited PDE regularity program while making the exact algebraic content and the remaining analytic obstructions fully explicit.

The principal mathematical question isolated by the manuscript is whether the exact exceptional-lattice quadratic form identified here can be derived with a positive coefficient from an effective dynamics and controlled by a genuine a-priori estimate. A positive answer would give the construction PDE significance; a negative answer would sharply delimit the scope of the exceptional-symmetry mechanism. We regard that falsifiable boundary as an essential part of the contribution.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
