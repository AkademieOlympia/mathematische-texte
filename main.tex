\begin{abstract}
We propose a topological framework for the infrared fine-structure constant $\alpha^{-1}(0)$ based on the boundary kinematics of an $E_8$ gauge field theory compactified on a Complete Intersection Calabi-Yau (CICY) manifold. We decompose the inverse coupling into a primary $U(2)$ principal bundle volume $V_{\text{main}} = 4\pi^3$, a Hodge-orthogonal boundary projection $V_{\text{boundary}} = \pi^2 + \pi$, and a sub-leading spectral correction $\Delta\alpha^{-1} = \delta_{G_2} \cdot (2 + \sqrt{5})$ associated with the non-associative geometry of octonionic $G_2$-automorphisms. 

To establish the structural consistency of the low-energy effective field theory, we evaluate the gauge sector compactification via two topological conditions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item \textbf{The Equivariant Lift Gate ($\mathbf{16}$-Fermion Sector):} Under the vanishing conditions $H^0 = H^2 = H^3 = 0$ on the twisted bundle $V \otimes L$, the equivariant Lefschetz index locks the chiral spectrum to exactly 3 generations across the character sectors of a discrete $\mathbb{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_3$ isometry.
    \item \textbf{The $\Lambda^2$-Cohomology Gate ($\mathbf{10}$-Higgs Sector):} Because the global Euler characteristic vanishes ($\chi(\widetilde{X}, \Lambda^2 V) = 0$), we apply the Koszul resolution on the ambient product space $\mathbb{P}^2 \times \mathbb{P}^2$ via the Bott-Borel-Weil theorem. We show that Wilson-line symmetry breaking induces a rank defect in the coboundary matrix $M_s$, projecting out mass-free color triplets ($m_{\text{triplet}} = 0$) while preserving a single light electroweak Higgs pair ($m_{\text{doublet}} = 1$).
\end{enumerate}
Furthermore, we utilize the Lean 4 interactive theorem prover (using Mathlib4) as an automated type- and charge-consistency checker. Lean 4 formally verifies the internal arithmetic constraints, the $\phi^3 = 2 + \sqrt{5}$ golden ratio identity, and the exact charge-conservation laws on the $E_8$ weight lattice. The complete differential-geometric stack is formulated as an integrative, structurally constrained framework for precision coupling unification.
\end{abstract}
